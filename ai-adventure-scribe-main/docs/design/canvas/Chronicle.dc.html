CHRONICLE · ABYSSAL DESCENT · SESSION 3

Ropes Cut from Below

The expedition reached the eleventh ledge at dusk, if dusk still meant anything this far down. The rope held. The piton, hammered in by those who came before, groaned once and held too. It was the torn page that troubled the Veteran: “Day 9. The ropes were cut from below.”

He went down anyway, hand over hand, sword drawn, boots slick on wet stone. On the ledge, eight small lights opened in the dark and turned toward him.
