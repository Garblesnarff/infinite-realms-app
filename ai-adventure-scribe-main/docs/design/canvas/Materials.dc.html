Speed. SVG noise filters are costly on large areas. For production, render walnut, felt and parchment once to small WebP tiles (we make them; no paid packs) and keep the SVG only for the deckle edge.
Truth. The die, the stamp and the tier words only show what the rules engine returns. Animation never picks a number.
Calm by default. One loop at a time near the eye line (the current tent card). Candle and motes stay at the edge. Reduce Motion stops all of it.
