Abyssal Descent

Terrifying · Claustrophobic · Sanity-breaking

The story

The party joins an expedition to descend the Infinite Chasm, a vertical cave system that supposedly reaches the center of the earth. What begins as scientific exploration becomes a nightmare as the depths reveal horrors that shatter sanity: impossible geometry, creatures that exist outside normal reality, and the crushing realization that something at the bottom is calling them down. Equipment fails, party members disappear or go mad, and the only way out is through.

Hard campaign: target numbers (DC and AC) start hidden. You can show them in Settings.
