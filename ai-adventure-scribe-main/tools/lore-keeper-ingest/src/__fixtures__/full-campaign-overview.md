# The Sunken Meridian

**Campaign Type / Genre:** Exploration / Seafaring Fantasy
**Tone:** Wondrous, Perilous, Hopeful
**Difficulty:** Hard
**Player Level Range:** Start **1** → Finish **8**
**Estimated Length:** 8-12 sessions

**Core Premise:** A drowned meridian line of ley power has resurfaced off the coast, and every chart in the admiralty is suddenly wrong. The party sails the shifting straits aboard a borrowed brig, racing a cartographers' guild that would sell the new map to the highest bidder, while the sea itself keeps redrawing the route home.

## The Story

The campaign opens in a harbour tavern, where a drowned sailor walks in out of the rain carrying a chart dated next week.
