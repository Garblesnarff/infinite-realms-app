# Twelve Bells

**Campaign Type / Genre:** Mystery / Gothic Horror
**Tone:** Tense, Mournful
**Difficulty:** Medium
**Player Level Range:** Start **3** → Finish **3**
**Estimated Length:** One-shot — 1 session (2–4 hours)

**Core Premise:** The abbey bell has rung twelve times for a thirteenth year running, and every villager who hears the final toll forgets one name. The party has until midnight to climb the bell tower, face the keeper who collects the names, and decide which single memory the village can afford to lose.

## The Story

The session opens at dusk on the last day of the year, with the first toll already sounding over the rooftops.
