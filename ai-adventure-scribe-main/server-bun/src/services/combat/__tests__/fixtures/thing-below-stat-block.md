**10. The Thing Below (Avatar) (CR 12)**
*   *Visual:* Shifting geometry, eyes, tentacles, starlight.
*   *HP:* 200, *AC:* 19.
*   *Attack:* +7 to hit, range 60 ft, 10d10+4 psychic
*   *Speed:* 30
*   *Size:* huge
*   *Resistances:* force
*   *Immunities:* psychic
**Opener:** Starlit Lash | effect: strike | tell: The geometry folds toward one point and a tendril of starlight unspools from the dark. | telegraph: none | counterplay: Keep out of reach of the tendril, or drop behind cover before it lands.
**Turnabout:** Gaze of the Void | effect: frighten wis | tell: Eyes open across its whole shifting body and every torch in the chasm dims. | telegraph: none | counterplay: Hold to the rope and look at your feet; a steady mind breaks the gaze.
**Finale:** Reality Break | effect: burst int | tell: The air bends, and your own thoughts echo back a half-beat wrong. | telegraph: The Thing Below begins to hum, and the walls take on a deeper shade of dark. | counterplay: Take the Dodge action while it is telegraphed; the rift folds past anyone who braces.
